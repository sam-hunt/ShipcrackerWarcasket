# Compat art roots (Mods/<Name>/)

Version-independent art for an optional mod, opened by that mod's `IfModActive` entry in
`LoadFolders.xml`. A variant of a main-tree texture goes at the main tree's exact relative
path (a later load folder wins a shared path); nothing here is referenced by a new name. The
gating rules are in `1.6/CLAUDE.md`.
